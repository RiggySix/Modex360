# Modex 360

An all-in-one tool for Xbox 360 consoles (including JTAG/RGH/Softmod), built on top of Horizon, with a new category of **realtime editors** for live, in-game modding as well as Horizon's game save and profile editors. 

I recently got back on the 360 scene after being gone for over a decade and I started off making an AIO tool just for modded Xbox 360s but then I thought why not add non-modded Xbox tools and I came across the Horizon v2 source code by unknownv2 so I thought I would save time by just building on top of that to make the ultimate AIO tool for both modded and retail Xbox 360s.


## Requirements for the RTE tools

- A JTAG/RGH/Softmod modified Xbox 360 console, reachable on your local network.
- Xbox 360 XDK installed on your computer
- JRPC2.xex and xbdm.xex on your Xbox 360

## Getting Started

1. Download pre-built package in the Releases.

-or-

1. Clone the repository.
2. Open the solution in Visual Studio and build.
